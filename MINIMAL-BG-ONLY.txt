IF YOU ONLY WANT TO FIX THE BACKGROUND FIRST
=============================================

In style.css find these two lines (near the bottom):

    url('./menu-background.png') !important;
  background-size: 100% 100% !important;

Change them to:

    url('./MenuBG.JPG') !important;
  background-size: cover !important;

Save, commit, push.

Then open the site in a PRIVATE/INCOGNITO window.
