BLØKS – Menu background → MenuBG.JPG
====================================

Files in this zip
-----------------
• replace-cinematic-block.css  → replaces the old cinematic block in style.css
• service-worker.js            → full replacement (uses MenuBG.JPG + cache v14)

What you must do
----------------
1. Put your image in the ROOT of the repo as:
     MenuBG.JPG

2. Open style.css
   - Find the block that starts with:
       /* ============================================================
          BLØKS CINEMATIC MENU ART BACKDROP
   - Delete that entire block
   - Paste the contents of replace-cinematic-block.css in its place

3. Replace service-worker.js with the one from this zip
   (or at minimum change menu-background.png → MenuBG.JPG and bump
    CACHE_NAME to 'bloks-cache-v14')

4. Commit & push both files + MenuBG.JPG

5. Hard refresh:
   - Open the site in a private/incognito window, OR
   - Unregister the service worker in DevTools → Application

After that the menu will use MenuBG.JPG (cover, no stretch) and the
title will stay hidden with the controls at the bottom.
