HOW TO PLAY
The goal
Guide falling blocks into complete horizontal lines. Full lines clear and score points. The board fills up as you go — clear lines to keep it under control. The game ends when a new piece can't fit at the top.
Touch controls
Swipe left or right to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to hard-drop, swipe up to hold. On-screen buttons are also available — turn them on in Settings.
Keyboard controls
←→ move   ↓ soft drop   ↑/X rotate   Z rotate back   Space hard drop   C hold   P pause
Scoring
Clearing more lines at once scores far more: a single line is worth 100×level, a Tetris (4 lines) is worth 800×level. Chain clears back-to-back without a break for combo bonuses, and land consecutive Tetrises for a back-to-back multiplier.
Hold & ghost piece
Hold sets the current piece aside for later — use it once per piece. The faint outline below your piece is the ghost: it shows exactly where a hard drop will land.
